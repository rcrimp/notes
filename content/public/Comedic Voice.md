---
title: Comedic Voice
---

- Your voice can be really you, an exaggeration, or a character. But it must be consistent. 
- Contradicting yourself reduces you to a shallow rhetoric machine. No underlying relatability. 
- When you define your voice, your jokes get easier to write. A clearly defined personas do the writing for them. So when you find your voice, it is both easier to write material, and easier to write material unique to you.
- Who we think we are doesn’t matter, but how we are perceived as. 
	- The chasm between the two is known as “delusion.”
- Ask 10 people who you’re close with, and 10 people who barely know you to describe you in a few words (without sparing your feelings)
