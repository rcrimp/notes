# Roles
1. Producer
	- books venues, hires talent, sells tickets
2. Stage Manager
	- orchestrates the live event
	- communicates between sound tech, front of house, ushers, stage talent, box office, etc.
3. MC
	- Breaks the ice, keeps the night flowing
	- Smooths over any hiccups
- For small shows, one person may do all 3 roles!

# This Is Your Captain Speaking

- Reassure people that everything will be fine
	- Everything is under control
- Tell people what they already know
	- *"This is flight NZ123. Departing 1600 hours. Arriving in Portugal 0700, local time. Expect some turbulence, nothing major. Snacks served in 2 hours. Drinks available at all times. Please fasten your belt for take-off and landing. The toilets are in the back."*
	- Introduces (almost) zero new information.
		- Most is well known, or could be easily guessed.
	- This establishes rapport and trust.
		- The person in charge, is on the same page as me.
- As stage manager / MC
	- same applies.

# Professional Checklist
1. Arrive early
2. Greet everyone
	- bar staff
		- ask if there are other gigs to plug
		- drink specials to push
		- house music
	- box office
		- confirm number ticket sales
	- sound tech
		- early mic check
	- other comedians
		- offer mic check
	- members of the general public
		- ticket holders and strangers
3. Prepare the room
	1. remove distractions
	2. arrange chairs
	3. sound check
	4. house music
