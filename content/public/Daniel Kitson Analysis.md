## Speech Style
Blend of two speech modes.
1. casual anecdote driven speech
2. poetic rhetoric at break neck, hell for leather speeds.

1. Knock about jokes with simple language.
2. Elaborate imagery, dense with detailed description and linguistically sophisticated eloquence.

## Story Premise
- Beging with the premise
	- clearly state what the story is about
	- it can be a bland premise
		- e.g. buying a chair...

## Refinement
- in the first draft a punchlines sucks, but the general structure is there
	- later drafts include an act out of an hightened emotion.
	- For example
		- the emotion of superiority over automated spellchecker.
- In a smug voice.

> I don't know who you think you're dealing with computer.
 
> who do you think you're dealing with Mr. Paperclip?

## Alliteration
Punchlines
> cunt cubed. Pigeon pussy

Act out emphasis 
> tik tok tin heart - fireworks talking

## Status
- Low status appearance.
- Uber geek. Non threatening.

- Humility to mock his appearance.
- Pedophile, homeless,
- Stutter, fat, weak, short, etc...
- Gives him room to make incredibly high status claims
- Knowledgeable, large vocabulary etc...
- He will also undermines his intellect by comparison to actual experts and sharing moments of ignorance.

## Pathos
- Emotional engagement.
- Attaches events/moments to emotion directly.
- He literally describes these massive complex emotions.
- Emotions that we all **want** to feel.

> Love, compassion, and the resolution of grief.

- Intimate because we don't just see the story as an observer, but we see a glimmer of the experience through the story tellers eyes.

- (Look at my me, look at my eyes).
- Look at my life, look through my eyes.
  
## Equation Jokes

- List, X, Y, Z...  
- One of them is Q  
- and its not X or Y  
> Parks are for children and pedophiles, and you are not a child

> Salt, grater, and my penis.  

> Cleaning out cage, mouse in bucket, dad not knowing mouse was in bucket, and needing to clean car.  
   
> Not knowing what a vicar was and not knowing what piss of meant told a vicar to piss off
