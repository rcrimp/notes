## Slip Box
one note, one idea, is only as valuable as it's context, not necessarily the context it came from, and possibly multiple contexts.

external scaffold for thinking.... or at least store ideas and unfinished thoughts

develop bottom up from atomic ideas to full manuscripts
just like standup comedy routines
do not need to brainstorm, just look in the slipbox for gaps, or existing connections

1. Fleeting notes - anything and everything all of the time, EVEN IF YOU DEEM THEM IRRELEVANT!!!
2. Literature Notes - write notes of what you read. distill content. keep it brief WITH BIBLIOGRAPHY!!!
3. Permanent Notes - SLIP BOX TIME
	1. incorporate your fleeting and literature notes into the slip box
		- atomic, clear write for someone else to read
		- does it fit neatly?
 		- does it support, add, contradict existing notes?
 		- can it be combined with existing notes for *insightful* ideas
 	2. Delete the fleeting notes
 	3. File the Literature notes for later reference
 
 gather notes, compile them into a document
