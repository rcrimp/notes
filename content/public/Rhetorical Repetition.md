## 8 Repetition Again
anaphora - Repeating the start "Mad world! Mad Kings! Mad composition!"
epistrophe - Repeating the end "I spake as a child. Understood as a child. Thought as a child."
symploce
epanalepsis - Rpeating the start at the end "Common sense is not so common"
inclusio

Anadiplosis - Repeating the end as the start "When I give, I give myself"
gradatio - repeated anadiplosis "fear leads to anger, anger leads to hate, hate leads to suffering"

quaternio terminorum
can be used to construct paradoxical sentences when one of the repeatitions is used in a different sense, i.e. antanacalsis

faith is better than nothing, nothing is better than faith.

auxesis
palindrome
epanados - Repeating in reverse "Fair is foul, and foul is fair"
	antimetabole - both an epanados and antithesis
	chiasmus - epanados at the 'passage' level
