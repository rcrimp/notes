# Dialogic Theory
Stand-Up is not strictly a monologue artform, it's a dialogue
In stand-up the audience reaction is usually be incorporated into the performance

Address the audience directly
Brechtian Alienation

## The Fourth Wall
1. Theatrical Comedy (and sketch)
	1. Actors never acknowledge the audience's reaction
2. Improv Comedy
	1. Actors address the audience for input, then transition to sketch actors in a scene
3. Stand-up Comedy
	1. The transition between acting and being present is rarely telegraphed

Stand up isn’t a monologue. The audience react to you, and you should react to the
