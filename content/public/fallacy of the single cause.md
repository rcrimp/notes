# Fallacy of the single cause
when you assume there's a single cause

when you assume there's a single explanation

when you assume there's a single interpretation

when you assume there's a single meaning

a word may have many meanings
a phrase may have many interpretations
a concept may have many explanations
an event may have many causes

these many options may be competing or harmonious.
e.g. many are true and relevant
or maybe only one is true
