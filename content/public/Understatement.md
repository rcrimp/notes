## diminutives
Many understatements follow the form:
It is ( just / only / maybe / kinda ) a ( little / tiny / bit / some ) … ( severe-thing )
I only need some minor surgery, since it’s just a teeny-tiny brain tumor

[Meiosis](public/Meiosis.md) - euphemistic understatement
[Litote](public/Litote.md) - negating the opposite

## Comedic Examples
> “How many stars are there in the milky way?   ... at least 12”

> "Cannibalism is frowned upon in most societies." 	- Roald Dahl

> “The thing about Heroin is... it’s a bit moreish” 	- Harry Hill

> “Tis but a scratch; merely a flesh wound” 		- Monty Python

## Conversational Examples
1. Feeling quite painful → Wee-bit sore
2. Feeling quite good → Not too shabby (litote)
3. Seriously annoyed → I’m not bothered (litote)
4. Serious problem → Spot of bother
5. Seriously ill → Bit poorly

## Tragic Examples
After suffering from frostbite, then choosing to die in the Antarctic:  
> “I’m just going outside and may be some time” - [Captain Lawrence Oates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Oates) 1912

> "Ladies and Gentlemen, this is your Captain speaking. We have a small problem. All four engines have stopped. We are doing our damnedest to get them going again. I trust you are not in too much distress” - [Captain Eric Moody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Airways_Flight_9) 1982
