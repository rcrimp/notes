title: MC Guidelines
---

### Rules
1. Have fun
2. Being **open and friendly** is more important than getting laughs

### First Words
1. Make them cheer before you start
> Make some noise Dunedin!
1. Sincerely thank and welcome the audience
> Thanks you for coming, 
2. Build hype
> Tonight is going to be a lot of fun
3. Affirm their actions
> You made the right choice to attend

### Show Structure
For many audience members it’s their first ever show, so inform them about what’s going on.
1. Show Format
> There are 6 comedians. Break after the first 3, etc…
2. Open mic
> It's an open mic so. It can be somewhat scary, so we're going to be supportive.
3. Hecklers
> Some of the comedians are quite new, please don't heckle them.

### Introducing performers
The gig is a house party, you’re the host, and we’re gonna have fun!

Introduce your old friends (comedians) to your new friends (audience).

Say their name last
> welcome to the stage… ELTON JOHN!!!”

### Closing the show

Thank everyone, bar staff, the other comedians

Follow **Dunedin Comedy** on Facebook and instagram
Website: _www.dunedincomedy.co.nz_

Make people feel as if they participated in a special, magical moment.

Thanks for supporting grass roots comedy, local talent.

Make them feel like the heroes for coming out, because they are.
