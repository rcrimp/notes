# Hedging
Don't hedge, commit.

Remove verbal filters: rude, blasphemy, swearing, political correctness
Like when you’re drunk or those videos of people on wisdom teeth medication

> “Get out of your own way” - Robin Williams

Be ok with self deprecation, or offend someone else

Goofy & Silly > Serious & Analytical

Make reality elastic. Playfully recontextualize.

Look for the ridiculous in the mundane

Play characters. Sassy Diva, Pranking rascal, cheeky scampy

Set up the humor for others		T-up a “that’s what she said” line

Learn to turn it on, turn it off. Choose to be funny, or serious.
