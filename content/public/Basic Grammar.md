## Words
- noun - person, place, or thing
- verb - action, doing, being...
- subject - protagonist perfoming the action
- object
	- direct
	- indirect
- predicate
	- - everything which isn't the object

## Sentences
- simple
- compound
- complex
- proposition - can be evaluated as true of false
- assertion - a proposition which is (claimed) to be true

incomplete sentences are called fragments
"improper" to use alone, but can be used for effect (or poetic emphasis)

## phrase
group of words
part of speech
- noun phrase
- verb phrase 
- adjective phrase - with the blue shirt
- prepositional phrase - with the blue shirt
- adverbial phrase - for twenty days

## clause
has both a subject and verb

### Main Clauses
1. independent
2. matrix

#### independent clause
complete sentence
SVO
> I ate the pineapple
- noun phrase: I
- verb phrase: ate the pineapple

#### matrix clause
not complete, lacks an object
needs a dependent clause


### dependent clauses
1. subordinate
2. dependent

Is not a complete sentence

####  subordinate clause
begins with subordinating conjunction
- because ... I wanted to
- while ... the salmon flopped
- unless ... 

#### relative clause

begins with relative pronoun
- that ... she saw last night
- who
- which
- whose 
- where

Restrictive relative clause
Non-restrictive relative clause
https://www.lexico.com/grammar/clauses

The relative pronoun refers to the subject in the previous clause
