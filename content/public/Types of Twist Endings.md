#### Types of twist endings
1. Incongruity resolution
	- **Confusion** becomes **Clarity**
	- Sometimes things don't make sense until the end of the story.
		- Bill and Ted 3  
	- Finding congruence is satisfying
2. forced reinterpretation
	- X becomes y
	- X were actually Y the whole time
3. Anti catharsis (tragedy)
	- A character unwittingly brings about the oppositie of their story goal.
	- Ironic turn. The character unwittingly causes the opposite of their story goal.
	- Anti goal.  Ironic endings prerequire a narrative goal.
