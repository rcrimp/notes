## Performance Style
1. Be [Surprising](Surprise%20Theory.md)
	1. Don't telegraph the punchline, keep it hidden
	2. Adjust your delivery to surprise (tone, volume, emotion) [Prosody and Rhythm](Prosody%20and%20Rhythm.md)
2. Write [Tags](public/Tags.md)
	1. A joke is never done, there's always another tag
	2. Consider an [Act Out](Act%20Out.md) for a tag
		1. i.e. repeat the punchline, but from the perspective of a character (or thing) within the joke
3. Don't be unjustifiably cruel
	1. [Punching Up](punching%20up.md)
	2. [Distance](distancing.md) yourself and the audience from the [Joke Target](Joke%20Target.md) to make laughter guilt free.
	3. [Schadenfreude](Schadenfreude.md) says cruelty is acceptable if it's warranted (karma, revenge)
	4. [Benign Violation theory](Benign%20Violation%20theory.md) A minor inconvenience could be funnier than cruelty
