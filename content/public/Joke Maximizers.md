## Performance Style
1. Be [Surprise Theory](public/Surprise%20Theory.md)
	1. Don't telegraph the punchline, keep it hidden
	2. Adjust your delivery to surprise (tone, volume, emotion) [Prosody and Rhythm](public/Prosody%20and%20Rhythm.md)
2. Write [[public/Tags]]
	1. A joke is never done, there's always another tag
	2. Consider an [Act Out](public/Act%20Out.md) for a tag
		1. i.e. repeat the punchline, but from the perspective of a character (or thing) within the joke
3. Don't be unjustifiably cruel
	1. [punching up](public/punching%20up.md)
	2. [distancing](public/distancing.md) yourself and the audience from the [Joke Target](public/Joke%20Target.md) to make laughter guilt free.
	3. [Schadenfreude](public/Schadenfreude.md) says cruelty is acceptable if it's warranted (karma, revenge)
	4. [Benign Violation theory](public/Benign%20Violation%20theory.md) A minor inconvenience could be funnier than cruelty
