# Distancing
Detach the joke subject from something serious.
Relates to the [Benign Violation theory](Benign%20Violation%20theory.md)

> “Tragedy plus time equals comedy.” - Steve Allen

> “Tragedy is if I cut my finger, Comedy is if you walk into an open sewer and die” - Mel Brooks

## Techniques
1. Time
	- not *"too soon"* 
	- wait at least 7 years for a tragedy
2. Geographic distance
	- Different country
	- A [Joke Target](Joke%20Target.md) who isn't in the room.
3. Severity
	- Tragedy vs mishap
4. Mood / Persona
	- Sarah Silverman uses a innocent cute persona to deliver vulgar jokes.
5. Hearsay
	- Another good technique is to offload the prejudicial material onto another indivdual
	- e.g. "My grandma said this racist thing...", then we can laugh at the bigot.

## Being Rude
- It's okay to be rude
- Cross the line
- Push the boundaries
- Prosecute the powerful
- Laughter as a coping mechanism
- Audience want to laugh, but they will stifle their laughter if it feels wrong
