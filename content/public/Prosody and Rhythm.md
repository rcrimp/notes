# Prosody and Rhythm
All comedy is about rhythm, comedians often talk about "timing" being essential, but there's so much more to the prosody of perfect joke delivery than just timing. The anticipation, the momentum, the tension, are all vital to good comedy, and are all controlled with rhythm.

[Finding your rhythm](public/Finding%20your%20rhythm.md) (Comedy Without Errors)

## musical comedy 
Music provides the perfect substrate for comedic rhythm, it's so perfect many professional comedians consider musical comedy to be cheating, but they're clearly just envious of those who can sing.

They do say that the secret to comedy is timing, but timing is only a small part of prosody “rhythm”, the technical term being prosody.

> comedy is all about the division of time, rhythms, the fall of language - Dylan Moran

Rhythm is easiest learned through practice delivery performance

## Enthusiasm
Delivery of a dad joke can be deadpan, or more like Tim Vine. Call it enthusiasm, call it attitude, call it whatever you like, but it is crucial to the successful delivery of all his jokes.

## Momentum
It's like music, you build momentum with your speech, a fast regular pace, and then you switch down a gear and crash abruptly at the punch line.
