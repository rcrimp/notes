---
title: Referential Jokes
---

Referential jokes rely on content, semantic and pragmatic. Can be translated interlinguisticaly, and intersemiotically.

In contrast, verbal jokes involve phonological realization of the lexical items. Require Metalinguistic awareness.
> referential jokes outnumber verbal jokes-Salvatore Attardo's, Linguistic Theories of Humor
