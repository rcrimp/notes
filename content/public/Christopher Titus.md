- Titus Method, The Code, The Formula
	- lay the foundation **WITHOUT** being funny
	1. write down just the facts
		- no jokes **DRY**
			- give yourself space to not be funny
			- takes 35 minutes of writing to get funny
		- **ALL** the details
	2. write a punchline for every fact/detail
		- 45 points (facts/details) => 45 jokes
		- punchline can go off story, off topic
