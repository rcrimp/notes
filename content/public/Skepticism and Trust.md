Distinction between skepticism and mistrust.
Scientific skepticism is healthy and encouraged.
Mistrust in scientific concensus is dangerous.
Radical skepticism.

Its ok for a scientist to consider or even believe in alternative theories. This is skepticism.

It's not ok for them to disbelieve concensus science. This is mistrust.

If their alternative belief is outside of concensus AND conflicts with concensus, this is dangerous.

If a doctor prescribed alternate medicine as a substitute for actual medicine, that is a cause for alarm. They don't trust the medical community.

But if a doctor prescribed both radiation, chemo AND fruits and veges because they believe natural foods have healing properties. That's ok. Even a healing crystal can have placebo effects at a minimum.
