## 🍀Writing Process
- Digital Garden's are often desribed with a gardening metaphor
- 🌱 Plant seedling 
	- Capture ideas as fleeting notes
		- sporadic thoughts and rough drafts
- 🌿 Nurture budding leaves
	- Maintain and groom note collection
		- clarify, categorise, and clean up
		- add, remove, join, and divide notes
- 🌲 Evergreen Note
	- Permanent reference notes
		- (reasonably) complete
- 🌲🌲🌲 Forest
	- Easy to update / edit / modify
	   -  Evergreen forest
		   - searching
		   - navigating
		   - building a set list

## 📄 Notes
- plaint text files in .md format.
	- Future proof
		- portable to any system
- Notes link to other Notes, collectively they make a knowledge graph (KG).
- Joke material should be atomic
	- a single joke or routine
	- If they grow too large they should be divided into separate Notes.
- Map of Content (MOC)
	- an index define the macro scale structure of the KG.
