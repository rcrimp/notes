---
title: contradiction
---
- Something which is *"logically"* false
	- False by virtue of its logical form
	- you can't be two mutually exclusive states
	- you cannot be alive *and* dead
- A statement that is necessarily false, due to internal logical incompatibility.
	- Self contradicting. Paradox, Oxymoron, Witticism, Irish Bull, …?
- Contradiction of terms, their definitions contradict eachother.
- Contradiction of facts, possibly but unlikely.
- Contradiction of ideas
- What doesn’t kill me, makes me stronger.
- I've done everything the Bible says — even the stuff that contradicts the other stuff! - Ned Flanders
- I can resist everything except temptation – Oscar Wilde
- I never said most of the things I said – Yogi Berra
- Many of these contradictions convey some “deep” truth, despite the untrue literal semantics.
- What's true in the premise is false in the punchline (or vice versa)
	- what Josh Ritchie calls a "false premise"
- Common in proverbs and literature
	- Less is more
	- Silence speaks volumes
	- Reach for the stars
	- A man’s reach should exceed his grasp, or what’s heaven for - Robert Browning
	- Too much of a good thing
	- friend to all is a friend to none - Aristotle
	- I want peace and I’m willing to fight for it - Hunter S Truman
	- Fighting for peace is like fucking for virginity - George Carlin
	- The liberals can understand everything but people who don’t understand them - Lenny Bruce
	- a lot of things just aren't true anymore
