More free form than traditional structures

## Ironic Boasting
- examples
	- "I graduated high school... not to brag"
	- "I ride on the bus... not to brag"
- Structure
	- **setup**: state something easily achieved
	- **punch**: claim it to be impressive 
- emphasize: false modesty, mock humility

## Ironic Condecension
- **setup**: claim an obvious falsehood as truth ([Honest Deceptions](Honest%20Deceptions.md))
- **punch**: mock those who don't beleive it
