# Pleonasm
- word redundancy
	- Tuna Fish, Burning Fire, Free Gift, Jewish Rabbi, Armed Gunman
	- Dishonest politician,
- Pleonasm is to Tautology as an Oxymoron is to Contradiction.
- Pleonasm is a redundant adjective or adverb.
	- Wet water
- Tautology is redundancy in a larger phrase, usually across a verb.
	- Water is wet.
