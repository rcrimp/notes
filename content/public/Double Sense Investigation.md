# double sense

Implication, Innuendo, puns, double entendre, 

What is the connection and distinction between an Euphemism and a pun?
Both involve 2 distinct senses

describe 'soundness' and 'relevance'
phrase level vs term level

**Euphemism**
1st sense, the literal sense is intended to be discarded, semantically present, irrelevant, unsound
2nd sense, the hidden sense is to be inferred, semantically absent, relevant
2 senses are incompatible, so the first is discarded.
> Sleep with the fishes

**Innuendo**
1st sense, the literal sense is vague, incomplete, semantically present, relevant, sound 
2nd sense, the hidden sense is to be inferred, semantically incomplete, relevant, sound
entailment between the 2 senses, both are kept.
> They spend ALOT of time together, if you know what I mean...

THERE ARE VARIOUS DIFFEREN PUNS TYPES IN THIS FRAMEWORK!!!!!!!!!!
despite the fact they rely on homophonic words
groan inducing bad puns don't force the listener to discard one of the senses.
Both senses are either simultaneously compatible, or independently possible.
> I lost all faith in my dentist after he said: brace yourself

both the figurative and literal interpretation of brace yourself are possible, and aren't mutually exclusive.

Good puns have a single sound interpretation, the 1st is usually discarded
> The only thing worse than raining cats and dogs... is hailing... Hitler
