Reinterpreted an acronym.

My first memory of this joke was from primary school, a fellow classmate claimed he was an FBI agent, specifically a Female Body Inspector. I knew it was a joke even though I didn't know what FBI really stood for.

> DNA stands for damn near attractive - Jerry Seinfeld

> Golf could only stand for Get Out; Leave Family - Jerry Seinfeld
