# Deepity
Daniel Dennet has scorn for a certain class of these quotes
he considers them pseudo philosophy, and refers to them by the diminutive "deepity"
a quote that appears to have philosophical depth but is merely a trite observation coupled with shallow wordplay.

superficial equivocation

Many people, namely propositional logicians, find these "deep" quotes empty of information, they conclude they're either
- obviously tautological
- paradoxically contractory
- sometimes both!

> Why stop dreaming just because you're awake

There are a few elements here. First, it's an interrogative, which persuades the reader to pause for thought, and consider an answer. A common element of philisophical depth is something which is "though provoking". Easy.

The other element is a the ambiguous verb "to dream"; meaning either to imagine nonsense while unconscious, or imagine aspirations, lofty desires.

The quote implies a dichotomy between chasing your goals AND dreaming in your sleep, but clearly there is none.  

This is a quote what Daniel Dennet would call superficial
