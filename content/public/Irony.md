# Irony
- Something contrary expectations
- A contradiction between reality and our view of reality
- Coincidence is common in irony, but not necessary
- There are at least 3 distinct concepts known as "irony"

1. [Verbal Irony](public/Verbal%20Irony.md)
2. [Situational Irony](public/Situational%20Irony.md)
	1. includes: cosmic, tragic & poetic irony
3. [Dramatic Irony](public/Dramatic%20Irony.md)
