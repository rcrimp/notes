## Paradox? Antithesis?
I would much rather hate myself than hate other people.
Unfortunately, I don't know who I am so I hate everyone on principle.

I would much sooner save my enemies than kill my friends.
Unfortunately, I have no friends so I save everyone on principle.

I would much rather be rude to my friends than be polite to my enemies.
Fortunately I have no enemies, so I'm rude to everyone on principle.
