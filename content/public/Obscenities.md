https://www.bsa.govt.nz/research-and-reports/research/all-research/what-not-to-swear-the-acceptability-of-words-in-broadcasting-2010/

General public survey of swear word acceptability, broadcast after 8:30pm
Including the scenario of Stand-Up Comedy

### General demographics
- Males are more accepting
- Younger are more accepting
- Non-religious are more accepting
- Pakeha (European descent) are more accepting
