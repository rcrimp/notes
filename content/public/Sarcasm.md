---
title: Sarcasm
---
- Separate from [Verbal Irony](public/Verbal%20Irony.md)
	- Sarcasm is a sassy attitude
	- Verbal irony is saying the opposite of what you mean
	- Sometimes they're used simultaneously
- Conveyed through vocal inflection
	- monotone or fluctuating tone
	- lower pitch
	- slow pace
	- eye-rolling
- condescending, bitter, harsh, taunting, mockery...


## Rhetorical Question
> “Is that what you’re wearing?”

## Exclamation
> “Oh... you’re wearing THAT!!”

## Verbal Irony
> “I’m Glad you’ve made an effort and dressed up”
