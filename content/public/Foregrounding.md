## Foregrounding
Through the lense of artistic appreciation noticeable deviations (deliberate or not) are the foreground element, where the background is the language.

Deliberate foregrounding is often seen in poetry, the use of rhythm, rhyme and metaphor are deliberate deviations from standard speech

The pun is also a deliberate deviation

Repetition is a deviation.
