# Surprise Theory
Aristotle was right when he identified the secret to humour as surprise.

> “The secret to humour is surprise” - Aristotle

> "There is no joke without surprise" - George Carlin

Although, everyone already knows this. 
A joke isn't funny the second time, it's predictable, it lacks surprise.

## Expectations
To surprise someone, deviate from their expectations, be unpredictable, deviate from the parameters defined by convention.

Play peekaboo - object permanence
Tickling - you cannot tickle yourself, nor does tickling at regular intervals work.

## Impact
There is a conceptual metaphor that a joke which is funny has "impact"
the joke "hits" us
it's called a "punch" line
a failed joke is "weak"
The surprise must be sudden
it must catch us off guard
jolt
