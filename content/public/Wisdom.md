# Wisdom
Wisdom is telling people what they already know.

For example, the secret to humor is surprise.

> There we are, that's ruined the show for everyone, that's bankrupt Netflix.

You already knew that was the secret because the second time you hear a joke it's not funny, because it's not surprising, it's predictable.

## other people's opinion on wisdom
> Buddha: he taught virtue, mindfulness (undirected focus), and wisdom.

> Wisdom is a matter of making choices, such as letting go of lesser pleasures for the sake of greater ones.
