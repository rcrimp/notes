What we know and what we don’t know.
Our awareness of our own knowledge 


> “There are known knowns, there are things that we know that we know
There are known unknowns, that is to say that are things that we know we don’t know
There are also Unknown unknowns, there are things that we don’t know that we don’t know”
--- Donald Rumself March 2003

# Epistemology of ideas
### Known Knowns
Things one is aware they know. 
The sky is blue
	I am here

### Known unknowns
Things one is aware they don’t yet know (but can)
	The colour of Venus’s atmosphere… 
how many cars parked outside

### Unknown Unknown
Things one is unaware they don’t yet know
	The colour of Russell's Teapot
	Wouldn’t have known that we don’t know
	The author of this sentence (me) is anosmic, he (I) can’t smell

### Unknown Knowns
> Things we know, but we don’t even know that we know them. 
> We are unaware that we know them.
> This is ideology, not our explicit thesis. They are embodied in knowledge and materials… implicit ideological prejudices and presuppositions. Even though you are not aware of them, they still regulate your behaviour… 
--- Slavoj Zizek 

# Knowable vs Unknowable
Known knowns and known unknowns are intrinsically knowable, can’t be unknowable.

Knowable unknown unknowns
Things that we might oneday learn of, and also become aware of.

unknowable unknown unknowns
Things which are outside the possibility of ever knowing about… you cannot even speculate here.

Knowable unknown knowns
Most implicit ideology that we have identified at least.

unknowable unknown knowns
Perhaps implicit cognitive programming… maybe something at the core of consciousness awareness. Something like you cannot look at your own eye, or bite your own teeth… you cannot ever know about the implicit functionality of your own mind.
