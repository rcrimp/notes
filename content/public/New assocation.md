Jokes that forming an association between concepts that didn't exist prior  

## Stewart Lee
associates anarchy and orienteering.  
  
> Orienteering with napalm death  
> It wasn't mainstream orienteering  
> It was first wave, proto-speed metal, anarcho-punk orienteering.  
> We had maps, but all the boundaries were crossed out.  
  
## Adjective Modifiers
- Linguistically done with adjectives to modify a noun phrase
	- anarchist cartographers cross out boundaries on maps
	- Cartographer with arachist tendencies cross out boundaries on maps.  
  
X + Y = Z  
  
Z is the association, defacing maps, that connects X to Y
