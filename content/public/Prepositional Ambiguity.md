## Prepositional Ambiguity
> I saw the man **on** the hill **with** a telescope

> I shot an elephant in my pajamas. How he got into my pajamas I'll never know. - Groucho Marx

> Alice: “Even **in** high heels, I’m still shorter than my dad”
> Reuben: “Why does your Dad wear high heels?”
