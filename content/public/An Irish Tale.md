## An Irish Tale
> My uncle died
> 	HE LEFT ME MONEY
> I bought a house
> 	A PUBLIC HOUSE (pub)
> There was only one bar...
> 	A MILE LONG!
> They didn't serve pints...
> 	ONLY BUCKETS!
> There was only one barmaid...
> 	FOR EVERY MAN!
> There was no furniture
> 	ONLY BEDS
> The guards came...
> 	A BEAN GARDA! (female police) (Bean si - female fairy)
> She was wearing glasses...
> 	AND NOTHING ELSE!
> She took me name  
> 	AND ME NUMBER
> They hand cuffed us  
> 	TO THE BEDS
> Then gave us jobs    
> 	BLOW JOBS
> She took us to jail..
> 	IT WAS FULL OF HOOKERS!
> They cost a fiver...
> 	I HAD A TENNER!
> There was a plant...
> 	A HASH PLANT!
> It had no leaves...
> 	I SMOKED THEM ALL!
> and that's the end...
> 	TILL NEXT WEEK!
