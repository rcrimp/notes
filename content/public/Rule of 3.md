- The Comic Triple
- A form of misdirection. Establish then break a pattern.
- A list of 3 items is the quickest way to establish a pattern, and then break the pattern.
- If I say, “Monday, Tuesday…” you can guess the third.
- If I say “Monday Tuesday Banana”, it ends Surprisingly
- The secret to humor is surprise. Which is self evident that a joke needs surprise.
- Because a joke isn’t funny the second time you hear it, it’s predictable.
- Only if a joke is unexpected can it be surprising, and therefore be funny.
- Comic Triple has three moves

1.  Establish   
2.  Reinforce
3.  SURPRISE  
   
- normal, normal, RANDOM
- normal, normal, ALARMING
- short, short, REALLY LONG
- general, general, SUPER SPECIFIC
- funny, FUNNIER, FUNNIEST

## Narrative Structure

Many street jokes involve three stock characters.

Three people of different nationalities enter a pub. The first behaves sensibly, the second also sensibly, and the third acts in a manner both stupid and stereotypical to their nationality, bringing shame to themselves ant their entire country.

You can Easily embed the Comic Triple into your own stories, by repeating three similar events, the first two expected, and the third unexpected.

My mum said something polite, my sister paid me a compliment, and my dad said something horrifically embarrassing.

The first two events establish a pattern. A certain expectation is built. strengthens the final event as points of contrast.

The rule of 3 is used by journalists to prove a trend.

### Tricolon
In literature, the repetition of three is called the Tricolon

It is a dramatic speech heightener, it adds emphasis, gravitas, and a feeling of magnificence.  

> the truth, the whole truth and nothing but the truth.

> Father, Son and the Holy Spirit

> Life, Liberty and the Pursuit of Happiness

> Truth, Justice, and the American Way.

> eat, drink, and be merry

alliterative
> wine, women, and song

Chris Rock is a master of repetition for emphasis.

Here’s Chris Rock on Fathers and Mothers.

> Cause people don’t give a fuck…. Nobody gives a fuck about Daddy. Everybody takes Daddy for granted. Just listen to the radio. Everything’s ”Mama. Dear Mama. Always loved my Mama.” What’s the Daddy song? Papa was a Rollin’ Stone - Chris Rock

The emphasis Chris Rock builds builds contrast. 

It’s the disparity of the juxtaposition that emphasises feelings of irony, or hypocrisy.  
  
## Bathos
If you can build emphasis, then you can use Bathos

Build dramatically towards something grand, but instead lead to an anticlimax.

They came… They saw… They did a bit of shopping.
