Corrective Judgement
Reality   → 	Exaggeration		→	“Reality is not that severe”
Reality   → 	Understatement	→	“Reality is more severe”
Reality   → 	Irony					→	“Reality is the opposite”

Innuendo → “... I know what you really mean”  
Allusion → “I saw that movie too”  
Double entendre → “I understand the dual meaning”
Analogy → “I understand the connection now”
