## Writing Challenge
Standup is about difficulties and challenges in life.
Use personal experience for a common struggle.

1. Think of a **specific** difficulty you have faced.
	- personal experience, struggle
	- could be minor or major
	- frustrated, overwhelmed, stressed
	- difficult person, coworker or boss
	- difficult decision
	- parties, networking events, group activities, 
		- choosing an outfit
	- personal failures
2. With your **specific** difficulty
	1. list the emotions and thoughts that you experienced as you were dealing with this challenge.
	2. What did you feel? What did you think? How did you react?
3. Think how other people may have similar challenges.
	1. What are common thoughts, feelings, and reactions?
