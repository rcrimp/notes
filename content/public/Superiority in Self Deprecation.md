## Superiority in Self Deprecation
Self deprecation can be understood under the superiority theory. One can mock their past mistakes, taking a superior stance above their past self as their current self has learned from the mistake and is now aware of what they were previously ignorant too. For example, by mocking an embarrassing fashion choice, your are indirectly saying that you are better than your past self as you wouldn't grow a mullet again.

Dissociating you current self from your past self is not an instinctive behavior, a child lacks the maturity to be amused at their past mistakes, they are instead embarrassed.

Sometimes we are more forgiving of our own mistakes if they cause us to suffer, than if another caused us to suffer. For example, accidentally cutting one's own fringe may be funny, but paying a hairdresser to fuck it up may cause rage.

> Ridiculing a picture of yourself in a certain predicament - Albert Rapp
