## Irish Bull
> Policeman: "Say you! If you're going to smoke here, you'll have to either put out your pipe, or go somewhere else."

> Go and see if I'm over there

> Oh, I never watch TV. I turn it off more than I turn it on.

> Irishman: "Be gorra I wisht I knew the place where I am goin' to die, and sure and I'd niver go there!"

> a man who was so tall that he had to climb a ladder to shave himself
