### Primary Neurotransmitters
- Glutamate and GABA excite and inhibit neurons

### Neuromodulators
- Dopamine rewards and attention, promotes approach behaviour
- Epinephrine (Adrenaline) regulates anger and fear, alerts and arouses
- Serotonin regulates mood, sleep, digestion
- Acetylcholine wakefulness and learning

### Other neurochemicals
- Cortisol stress response
- Testosterone regulates lust
- Estrogen libido mood and memory

### Neuropeptides
- Opioids (endorphins) regulate pleasure and stress, (euphoric runners high, pain intolerance)
- Oxytocin nurturing towards children and mates, blissful intimacy
- Vasopressin regulates pair bonding (protective and possessive love, physical safety and sexual jealousy)

### Mental illnesses
- Love = dopamine + serotonin + oxytocin
- Depression = low dopamine + low serotonin
- Schizophrenia = excess dopamine
- Anxiety = low dopamine
- Parkinson’s = low dopamine
- Mania / bipolar linked to excess serotonin
- ADD is linked with Serotonin

### Pair Bonding
- Cuddling releases Oxytocin and Vasopressin which are the pair bonding neuro chemicals.
- Pair bonding is a dangerous double edged blade, it’s the “catch feelings” disease, it is “falling” in love.
- Physical engagement engenders feelings of emotional attachment and blissful intimacy, feelings of dependability, safety and security… a wonderful feeling the opposite of loneliness.
- In a healthy relationship this feeling is mutually reciprocated, your safety is in their hands, and their safety in yours. One feels compelled to protect their spouse, ideally protect them from harm, but also protect them from sexual rivals.
- These feelings of jealousy and romantic possession can be expressed as abusive control.
- It makes you jealous of romantic rivals and protective towards your own partner, effectively controlling, preventing them from being with other people, sometimes preventing them from leaving the house alone without permission.
- The force is ensuring their safety, but it comes at the expense of her freedom.
- You desire for their safety, and your inability to trust her
