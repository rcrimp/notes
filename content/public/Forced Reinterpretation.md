# Forced Reinterpretation
- The process of "getting" misdirection jokes
- Misdirection jokes
	- bait and switch
	- pull back and reveal
	- switcheroo
- Relevant Theory
	- Script based semantic theory of humor
	- A listener assesses candidate interpretations for the same utterance and selects the most relevant candidate. Relevance predictions are correlated with low mental effort, plausibility and interestingness (positive cognitive effects in relevance-theoretic terminology). Human cognition performs this assessment of interpretations at a subconscious level. The speaker can predict one of the likelier interpretations (all other interpretations will be unconsciously discarded), then invalidate the interpretation in order to cause an incongruity and humour.
