# Part 2

- comedians can get laughs from sound alone, all intelligible content removed. 

Bob Mortimer have systematically broken their joke rhythms, playing all the right notes but not necessarily in the right order. By doing this, they are able to create a comedic style that is unique and often more humorous than the traditional comedic rhythm.

Bob Mortimer has an unpredictable and erratic delivery when telling stories, which is both chaotic and full of random details, illogical tangents, and unpredictable pauses. 

- Norm Macdonald similar to Bob
	- delivery is like a dementia patient who only remembers the punchline halfway through.
- Norm writes out his jokes in full, but makes them feel spontaneous with his pauses and misdirection.
	- like a jazz drummer, as he builds tension but never releases it and never gives the audience the traditional punch line. This often leaves the audience in a state of confusion and hysteria, as they are unsure of where to laugh and when to laugh
- rhythm and cadence
- Seinfeld and Melanie distinct rhythms
- To be a great comedian, one should strive to find their own rhythm that is as indelible as a signature.
