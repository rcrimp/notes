# Brief History of the Theory?
The instantaneous dissipation of emotional anxiety is pleasurable.

In 1711, Shaftesbury described the laughter as a release from constraint
> The natural free spirits of ingenious men, if imprisoned or controlled, will find out other ways of motion to relieve themselves in their constraint; and whether it be in burlesque, mimicry, or buffoonery, they will be glad at any rate to vent themselves, and be revenged on their constrainers

In 1878, Nietzsche described laughter occurring when fear (of death) transitions to safety.
> A momentary transition whilst fear resolves into it's opposite. From high-alert lethal-threat defense-mechanism of the animal towards the formality, security, tradition of the expected behaviour in the dependable man. - Human, All Too Human, 1878

 1928 - Freud (and Herbert Spencer) described laughter in the more practical light of social tension to relief. Nervousness, Hostility, awkward
sex and hostility were the only drives of repression that led to laughter

hostile jokes of cruelty [Superiority Theory](public/Superiority%20Theory.md)
obscene jokes of exposure 

Freud also regarded humor as a defense mechanism (1928)

1964 - Arthur Koestler described laughter happening when incongruity is resolved. The transition from confusion to understanding.

Theory of Incongruity as caused by emotive-speed being slower than logic-speed. The joke premise is emotionally charged, the punchline subverts or twists the premise. The logical mind realises the shift in meaning before the emotional mind, this delay causes an incongruous juxtaposition in sense

Emotional inertia / persistence - switches slowly
Intellectual reasoning and rational thought switches fast
Simultaneity, incongruity, synergy, biscotation, juxtaposition, script opposition,

## Nervous Energy
In situations of high nervousness a natural response is to laugh to alleviate the social tension

Steve Martin experimented with comedy without traditional punchlines, he attempted to keep increasing the tension, and anticipation without providing a release. He found the audience would eventually find their own release.
> “Punchline provides a release” Steve Martin
