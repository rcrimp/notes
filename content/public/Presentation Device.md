title: Presentaiton Device
---

- A *Presentation Device* is a [Figures of Speech](public/Figures%20of%20Speech.md), and should be considered separately from the *Logical Mechanism* (LM).
- The Logical Mechanism is the conceptual meaning inside a joke.
- [SnowClones](public/SnowClones.md) are obvious Presentation Devices
		- SnowClones are: fill in the blank(s) sentences:
			- `I like my lovers like I like my coffee ... ` 
			- `I like my X like I like my Y ... Z`
		- Closely related to [Anti-Proverbs](public/Anti-Proverbs.md)
- A *Presentation Device* is a grammatical construction, the specific syntax which defines element order. Elements are lexemes/phrasemes that bear content, they are conceptual containers.
- The element ordering influences the listener’s process of construal construction. Careful ordering of elements can manipulate the audience’s:
	- assumptions
	- expectations
	- inferences
in order to elicit surprise, reinterpretations, and hopefully laughter..
