Malapropism

Mondegreen - Mishearing. Conflating near homonyms. Scuse me while I kiss this guy

The use of an incorrect word with similar sound. Accidental homophonic pun.

Popular malapropisms are absurd, or have a provocative connotation for comic effect.

Important -> Impotent
Gutted -> Guttered
Election election erection
Flapper dress slapper dress
