---
title: Litote
---

Litote: an affirmative is expressed by the negative of its contrary

> Not bad.

> Not bad looking.

> Won’t regret it.

> Not uncommon.

> “Ferris does not have what we consider to be an exemplary attendance record” - Ferris Bueller's Day Off

> “you weren’t burdened with an over abundance of schooling” - Mal (Firefly)
