## Expanding a Story
- Like anecdotal speech
1. **Back Story**
	- Describe **WHO** you were
		- your younger self is a **character**
	- introduce the other characters with jokes
		- their relationship to you
		- their backstory
		- again, these jokes don't have to be relevant to the "story"
- Location
	1. Describe **WHERE** you were  
		- tell jokes about the location
			- even if they're not strictly relevant to the story
2. Then interactions between characters.
3. Ending / Resolution


### Catharsis
- Catharsis in 3 parts, the 
	1. Character with an objective  
	2. Crisis prevents objective
	3. Resolution  objective
		1. [[Police 10-7 DIY tradie]]
- Pathos + Characters in crisis = audience in crisis
- The resolution is relief for the character;
- the resolution is relief for the audience too
	- hearing a story causes the listener to neuroligcally experience the story as if it happened to them.
	- As if they were reliving a memory.  


## Aristotelian? Rhetoric
1. ethos: identity, perspective: THIS IS ME!
2. Logos: structure, parallelism, callback, setup-payoff.
3. Pathos: emotional investment: WHY YOU CARE!
