---
title: Self confidence correlates with humor
---

Studies with both adults and children show that humor correlates with self-confidence.

> People who have a positive self-concept and a more confident view of themselves as masters of their own fate laugh and enjoy humor more than those who do not feel in control. - John Morreal, Taking Laughter Seriously

- Infants who are not touched and held feel insecure and cry more
- Infants who are "mothered" feel more secure and laugh more
- If you make someone laugh will they feel in control, more secure, and confident?
- Is laughter the best medicine?
