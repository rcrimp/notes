an utterance in language can have many interpretations, due to ambiguity and vagueness.

> How to make a chicken salad?
> 1. make a salad
> 2. give it to the chicken

In traditional communication [Cooperative Principle](public/Cooperative%20Principle.md) we assume a single interpretation is intended. If there is more than one semantic interpretation, most are excluded, often at a cognitive level lower than our awareness.

The phrase "chicken salad" has two distinct interpretations, one more obvious. The less obvious interpretation is discarded unconsciously, only to be revealed with the punchline. [Forced Reinterpretation](public/Forced%20Reinterpretation.md)

Misdirection jokes: intentionally mislead the listener, make them forming an assumption or an expectation. Punchline falsifies the assumption, or subvert the expectation.
Force an interpretation then force a reinterpretation.
Encourage a prediction then invalidate that prediction.

> When god made me he threw away the mold… some of it grew back - Emo Philips

The word “mold” is ambiguous. It could refer to fungus or a hollow container. The assumption is coerced through the use of the common phrase "threw away the mold" derived from the Jean-Jacques Rousseau quote "God made me and broke the mold."

Initially the ambiguity goes unnoticed, until the punchline where the ambiguity is simultaneously revealed and resolved. This hijacking of the cognitive processes causes surprise, “the key to humour” - Aristotle

Falsify the assumption, and reframes or recontexturalises the utterance.

lexical ambiguity can be exploited for this comedic device,  but also structural ambiguity, as seen here:

> I shot an elephant in my pyjamas, how he got into my pajamas I'll never know - Groucho Marx

This is also known as prepositional ambiguity, or referential ambiguity
It is unclear as to whom the preposition “in” applies to
is the subject, “I” in pyjamas, or is the object “elephant” in pyjamas.
Clearly the more reasonable interpretation is assumed, only people wear pyjamas. The other interpretation is subconsciously discarded.
