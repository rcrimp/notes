Is it even possible to *learn* how to be funny? Are great comedians born or are they made? Ask any professional comedian and they will say it's a skill hard earned through experience; ask any comedy fan and they will tell you those comedians aren't funny.

> Many are willing to suffer for their art. Few learn how to draw - Simon Munnery

> Learn the rules like a pro so you can break them like an artist  - Pablo Picasso

> it is the hallmark of the desperately unfunny to study comedy - Eric Idle

> Defining and analyzing humor is a pastime of humorless people - Robert Benchley

## Talent vs. Experience
- Identifying the mechanics of a gun does not shoot the gun
- Being funny is a creative skill.
	- Any creative skill improves with experience
		- learning from your own 1st hand experience
- There are many very talented comedians.
	- Learn from talented / experienced comedians
		- learning 2nd hand from the experience of others

## Study What?
- [Humor Theories](public/Humor%20Theories.md) is a real academic disipline
	- Academics often study *"street jokes"*
	- Aspiring comedians often study their favourite comedians.

## Studying Jokes
- Explaining a joke isn't funny, but it can be educational
	- Dissecting jokes - [Killing the frog](public/Killing%20the%20frog.md)
