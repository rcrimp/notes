---
title: Chicken Joke
---

> Q: Why did the chicken cross the road?
> A: To get to the other side.

- This is not an anti-joke, it’s a pun (double entendre)
- The “*other side*” refers to both
	- the other side of the road
	- the euphemistic expression for the after life... 
		- “*crossing over to the other side*”
- Is it a suicidal chicken?
	- or is it merely walking?
		- or both?
