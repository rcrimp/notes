## Principles
1. Borrowed Creativity
	- remixing stuff
2. Capture Habit
	- brains are great at ideation and connecting concepts
	- brains are bad at reliable storage, especially in 2021, with information abundance
3. Idea Recycling
4. Projects over Categories
	- file ideas where the will be useful, where you will stumble on them later
5. Slow burn (not heavy lift)
	- incremental changes for parallel projects
6. Start with abundance
7. intermediate packets
	- chunking large projects
	- reuse chunks in different projects
8. You only know what you make
9. Make it easier for your future self
	- write for somebody else (future self)
	- Keep your ideas moving
10. Keep your ideas moving
	- anti perfectionism
	- it's not about perfect structure, it's about creative output
