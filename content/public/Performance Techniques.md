## Pauses
- Pauses can build suspense
- Pauses gives the audience room to laugh
- You must be comfortable with silence on stage
- Pauses allow the audience to predict (mispredict) the punchline
- Timing and Pace
- What you don't say is just as important.
	- The audience thinks quicker than you can speak
