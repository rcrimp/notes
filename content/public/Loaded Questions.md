# Loaded Questions
Very weak [Implicature](public/Implicature.md) by way of a [Presupposition](public/Presupposition.md) in a question

> Why do you hate Jesus?

Presupposes that Jesus is real

> Should a smack as part of good parental correction be a criminal offence in New Zealand? - 2009 NZ anti smacking referendum

Presupposes that corporal punishment can be good parental behavior

> What did your last slave die of?
> Do your parents know your gay?
> Are you always a dickhead
> Do you still beat your wife?

> When will the dinosaurs come back?
> Should we do something about all the gas stations selling nuclear arms and cheap abortions

A questions focuses the listener on a certain aspect of the sentence.  
  
The focus could be a distraction, away from the other aspects.
