# Heckler Put Downs

Put downs are considered public domain, it’s ok to use these if it saves you from a bad gig. You can rip them off completely, or make them your own, for example: 

“Don’t tell me how to do my job. I don’t come to your workplace and tell you how to sweep up”  \
 \
Attributed to Billy Connolly line but as early as Vaudeville.

Here are 4 variations on the same put down.



1. "I don't go to your job and slap the Sailor’s dicks out of your mouth”
2. "I don't go to where you work and slap the broom outta your hand"
3. "I don't go down to your job and salt the french fries”
4. "I don't go to where you work and analyse your investment portfolios”
