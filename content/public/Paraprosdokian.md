## Self Correction
- Epanorthosis is a rhetorical device which means an immediate correction in speech.
 
> He is the best warrior in the region -- nay, the entire country!

- The correction usually comes after
	- misspoken word
	- Freudian slip
	- or further clarification
		- (after something ambiguous or vague (incomplete))

> Guns don’t kill people, people kill people, and monkeys do too ... if they have a gun.(Eddie Izzard)

- Similar to accumulatio
	- which is when ones repeats the same thing in different words for effect

## Paraprosdokian
- Known informally as Bait-and-Switch
- Paraprosdokian is a kind of epanorthosis with two main differences.

### 1. It's Intentional
- In speech epanorthosis corrects something unintentional
- Paraprosdokian is an intentional correction
	- strong form of [Misdirection](public/Misdirection.md) for humorous effect

### 2. The miscommunication isn't overt
- In epanorthosis the incorrect word(s) are explicitly invalidated
- However, in paraprosdokian the invalidation is often *implied*

> I have the heart of a small boy... in a glass jar on my desk - Stephen King

- You don't say "a real heart"... you imply it.
- The initial interpretation of *heart* is figurative
	- however the second clause invalidates this interpretation in favor of a real heart.
- The invalidation is inferred by the listener (or reader) as they seek an interpretation of both clauses which are congruent with each other. The final interpretation arrived at is incompatible with the initial incomplete interpretation. Having such a mastery over language is considered witty.
- The first clause is intentionally misleading. The correction (correctio) is often called a *twist* as it changes the meaning of the first.

> I miss my wife's cooking - as often as I can”
— Henny Youngman

- Since paraprosdokian is humourous, the amendment usually involves irony, absurdity, taboo, or some other implicature. But sometimes the amendment merely invalidates the initial interpretation [Forced Reinterpretation](public/Forced%20Reinterpretation.md).
- In paraprosdokian an independent clause is followed be a dependent clause. 
- To achieve the effect both the grammar and delivery should indicate that the first clause has ended, that it is independent and complete. The second clause is then unexpected, amplifying the surprise of the forced reinterpretation.
