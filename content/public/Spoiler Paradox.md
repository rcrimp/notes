title: Spoiler Paradox
---
[https://www.google.com/amp/s/www.psychologytoday.com/nz/blog/thriving101/201108/the-spoiler-paradox%3famp](https://www.google.com/amp/s/www.psychologytoday.com/nz/blog/thriving101/201108/the-spoiler-paradox%3famp?authuser=0)  
  
- We enjoy stories more id we know how they end. Eg high grossing film titanic.  
- Mystery, suspense, uncertainty, actually distract from enjoying the narrative  
- The spoiler should be a simplified version of a complex narrative.  
- You can't spoil Romeo and jukiet because everyone knows at least a summary of the plot, and yet people still watch it  
  - Possibly relates to anchoring   
- The most popular stories told be people are parables. The boy who cried wolf. Everyone knows the ending, but we still tell them anyway.
