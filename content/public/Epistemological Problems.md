## Pinhole Problem
We cannot see the entire landscape. Our perspective is limited by ignorance.

## Metaphor Problem

## Criteria Problem
simplicity, optimally, beauty, elegance are seductive aspects of an explanation.

## Truth Problem
100% certainty is impossible. All "truths" have some chance of being wrong.

## lamp light problem

## hammer problem

## Meddler
