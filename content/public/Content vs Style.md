title: Content vs. Style
---
- A joke can be broken into content and style
- Jokes are usually categorised by content theme
	- observational, topical, political, sexual, toilet, dark, etc...
- To be most useful to a writer it's better to categorize jokes based on their `style`.
	- Once the style has been extracted, it can be repurposed for any theme.
